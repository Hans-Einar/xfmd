Scope and notation
The complete logical architecture uses contains edges, including isolated components.
R keys identify responsibilities on their immediate owners (owns). C keys identify
capabilities: contributes summarizes an owns + realizes path; provides records an
explicit offered capability. These annotations are not new SDL relations. Realizes is a
contribution, not proof of complete or verified realization. Ownership does not imply
provision, and provision does not imply realization. Rounded standalone capabilities
have neither a represented contributor nor provider. Keys are sorted within this
snapshot. Features and runtime allocation belong to other viewpoints.
