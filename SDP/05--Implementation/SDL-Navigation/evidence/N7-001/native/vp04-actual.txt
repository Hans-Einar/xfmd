Scope and notation
Every declared Interface is included, even without a consumer. Rectangles are explicit
Unit/Container consumers; rounded nodes are interfaces. Dashed consumes arrows

point from consumer to interface and mean designed use, not an observed call or
mandatory availability. No provider, Channel, transport, call order or mode necessity is
inferred. Units without consumes facts are outside this projection; VP02 shows the
complete logical structure.
