Scope and notation
Channel topology is partitioned into one diagram per declared Channel, retaining
every participant and binding. Repeated participants refer to the same source
declarations. The diagrams show explicit participation in one Mode, not runtime
delivery, transport or execution order. Sender arrows point from participant to
Channel; receiver arrows point from Channel to participant. Each arrow groups all
messages for that participant/channel/role; the register retains every binding and
source proof. Modes have no implicit inheritance.
