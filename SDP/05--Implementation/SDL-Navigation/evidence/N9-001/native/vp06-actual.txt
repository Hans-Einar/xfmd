Scope and notation
All declared Activities are retained, including isolated activities. Refines points from
detailed to abstract activity; depends-on points from dependent activity to its explicit
prerequisite. Decomposition and prerequisites are separate diagrams. Neither
relation inherits status or dependencies. No dates, durations, execution order or
calendar schedule are inferred.
Implementation status is a source claim, not test evidence or owner acceptance.
Addresses models Functionality coverage; delivers models a Feature contribution, not
complete delivery; illustrates links an example Scenario, not an executed test.
No Activities are declared. No delivery plan is inferred from Features, files or KanBan.
No activity prerequisites are declared. This does not prove independence.
No activity responsibility coverage is declared.
No activity Feature contributions are declared.
No illustrative Scenarios are linked to activities.
